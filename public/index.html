How it works

  1. 1

    Create your organiser account

    Sign up with your name, your school or organisation, and your email. Enter the code we email you, and you're in.

  2. 2

    Build the quiz

    Add questions one by one or upload them from Excel. Set when it opens, how long students get, and how it looks.

  3. 3

    Share the link and password

    Every quiz gets its own link and password. Students enter it with their details and start when the quiz opens.

  4. 4

    Watch live, export results

    See who is taking it, their progress and any warnings. Results are ranked and ready to download.

Everything a quiz day needs

Your own look

Pick the colours, font, logo and background for each quiz so it matches your school or event.

Fair for everyone

Each student gets the questions and options in a different order, or a random set from a larger pool.

Hard to cheat

Switching apps is recorded and can auto-submit, copying is blocked, and a name watermark covers the screen.

Timer you can trust

The clock runs on the server. Unfinished quizzes are submitted automatically when time is up.

Approved lists

Optionally upload the mobile numbers allowed in, so only registered students can enter.

You decide what students see

Show nothing, just their score, or their score and the correct answers after the quiz closes.
